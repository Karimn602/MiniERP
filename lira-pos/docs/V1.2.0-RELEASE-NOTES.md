## What changed

**Greaz's official menu is built in.** After enrollment the owner sees
"Greaz's official menu has not been loaded yet" on the Register, reviews the 33
items (27 menu items, 4 add-ons, 2 combo upgrades), chooses the VAT rate and
whether the printed prices include VAT, confirms, and loads it. See
`docs/OFFICIAL-MENU.md` for exactly what is created, what an existing database
keeps, and what the restaurant still has to confirm.

**Register.** New section tabs in printed-menu order (Fries, Appetizers,
Combos, Dessert, Combo upgrades). Dipper Chicken, Chicken Loaded Fries and the
Greaz Box ask for their free sauce or dips when added; the answer prints on
the kitchen ticket. Paid extras are their own priced lines.

**Fixed along the way (found by the independent review):** a barcode answer
that arrives after its order was already posted or cleared is now dropped
instead of being added to the next customer's order. This applied to every
scanned item before 1.2.0.

**No migration.** Installing 1.2.0 over 1.1.x keeps the database exactly as it
is and adds nothing to it until the owner chooses to load the menu. Take the
operator backup first anyway (`docs/OPERATOR-BACKUP-RUNBOOK.md`).

## Desktop acceptance (2026-11-23)

Real WebView2 evidence, in the sense CLAUDE.md defines: the actual Tauri
application against a real SQLite file, under a disposable identity, driven
over CDP on a loopback-only port (`127.0.0.1`), with rows read back from the
database. The three disposable binaries (`GreazPOS-MenuFresh/-MenuExist/-MenuTrn`,
identities `com.greazlb.pos.menufresh` / `.menuexist` / `.menutrn`) were built
from the development tree, whose application source is identical to `19fb4d2`.
Before each phase the process path, the port's owning process (the app's own
WebView2 child) and its loopback binding were checked. The owner launched each
app by hand; the app's ACL refuses a scripted window close.

* **Fresh / Training.** No sellable product before the load; owner-only

* **Restart.** The machine shut down unexpectedly before this phase. The
  MenuFresh database was checked first, on a byte-identical copy:
  `integrity_check` ok, 0 foreign-key violations, WAL checkpointed, 25
  migrations, 33 products / 33 distinct SKUs, one marker, receipt 1 posted at
  $48.50. Then: enrollment not repeated, still 33 products and one load record,
  sale persisted, no prompt, a second load refused with nothing changed.
* **Existing database.** A copy of the disposable V1.1 acceptance database (11
  sales, 3 shifts, 3 users, purchases, a recipe, returns). In-app: every
  pre-existing row of 17 tables unchanged by primary key; 31 created, Curly
  Fries and Water KEPT as same-named Register items (Replace not ticked), load
  record `created 31 / kept 2 / hidden 1`; only the ticked Double Burger left
  the Register, still active; 43 tiles = 14 existing − 1 hidden + 30 new
  sellable. Offline, against the untouched source, every column of 276
  pre-existing rows in 36 tables, credentials included: unchanged except
  Double Burger's `show_on_register`/`updated_at` and the owner's
  `users.updated_at`, which is the ordinary sign-in reset
  (`auth.rs`, `UPDATE users SET failed_attempts = 0 …`), not the load.
* **Isolation.** MenuFresh and MenuExist files unchanged by the Training run;
  the protected `com.greazlb.pos` and `com.greazlb.pos.training` databases
  byte-identical (name, size, mtime, SHA-256) to the fingerprint taken before
  any V1.2 work, re-checked at the end. They were never opened.

**Failures during the campaign were test-harness errors, not application
defects.** Each was corrected so the expectation is derived independently,
not loosened, and the affected phase was re-judged or rerun:

1. Fresh, tab order: the two theme buttons also carry `aria-pressed`, so a
   regex anchored on `^All` failed against a correct order.
2. Existing database, run 1 aborted: the snapshot read `users.*` and
   `users.rowid`; the read guard correctly refuses credential columns and the
   rowid to every renderer tier, owner included.
3. `product_uoms` "changed": the whole table was compared, so the 31 new units
   counted as changes. Now compared by primary key, plus "every added unit
   belongs to an official item".
4. "33 official products": wrong on an existing database by design — two
   same-named items are kept, not duplicated. Now must equal the preview's
   `create` rows, agree with the load record, and leave the kept items on the
   Register.
5. "32 tiles": a fresh-store number. Now derived from the pre-load catalogue.

For the existing-database rerun, MenuExist was restored byte-for-byte from the
untouched source; run 1's database and screenshots are kept in
`dev/menu/evidence/exist/`.

**For the owner:** on a database that already has items, a same-named item
keeps ITS price. In the test copy Water stayed at $6.50 against the printed
$0.50. That is the never-overwrite rule; tick "Replace" in the dialog, or fix
the price in Products afterwards.
