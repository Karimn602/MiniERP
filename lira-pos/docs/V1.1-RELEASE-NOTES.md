## Upgrading an existing installation

Installing 1.1.0 over 0.1.0 keeps the database. On first start it applies
migration 025, which:

* adds "Sold at the Register?" and "Allow stock to go below zero" to every item;
* **hides from the Register any item priced at $0.00 that is stocked or used as a
  recipe ingredient** (e.g. Meat, Potato Bun). Nothing is deleted or deactivated;
  the owner can switch any of them back in Products;
* starts the owner's cost/stock-policy audit log;
* restates no sale, purchase, return, shift or stock figure.

**Take the operator backup (`docs/OPERATOR-BACKUP-RUNBOOK.md`) before
upgrading.** Rolling back to 0.1.0 afterwards needs that backup.

## What changed for the shop

**Register**
* The cart is about 45% of the screen, one line per item; 10 lines show at
  1366×768 and the rest scroll inside the cart. Totals, cash boxes and Post Sale
  never move.
* **F5** = post the order now in exact **USD** cash. **F6** = exact **LBP** cash.
  If the cashier already typed part of the payment, the shortcut adds only the
  remainder. A held or repeated key cannot create a second sale. F5 never
  reloads the till.
* Ingredients and stock-only items no longer appear as $0.00 tiles.

**Products and stock (owner)**
* A barcode is optional for every item. SKUs are still generated automatically.
* "Sold at the Register?" — choose *Inventory only* for ingredients. They stay
  purchasable, countable and usable in recipes.
* **Manual Cost** now works on stocked items too. Sales of the item are costed at
  that figure until the owner changes it or switches back. The stock on hand is
  still valued at what it cost to buy, and past sales keep their cost. Every
  change is logged with the owner's name. Only the owner can set it.
* **Allow stock to go below zero** is set per item by the owner (it used to be a
  till setting anyone could switch on). A delivery received while stock is
  negative is valued at its invoice price.
* Greaz still does **not** take ingredients out of stock when a recipe item is
  sold. Recipe costs are estimates; purchases and stock counts manage the
  quantities.

**Returns**
* Every role — cashier, manager, owner — can refund a sale. Returns → type the
  receipt number → choose items and quantities → check the refund → Post return.
* Limits are unchanged: only items on that receipt, only what has not already
  come back, only through the way the customer paid, never more cash than the
  drawer holds.
* If a refund's answer is lost (the till closed, locked or crashed mid-refund),
  Greaz keeps the exact refund on record. Next time, Returns shows "A return …
  did not confirm" with a **Resolve** button: Greaz checks whether it went
  through. If it did, it says so and nothing is paid again; if it did not, you
  can send the *same* refund again or discard it. A new refund on that receipt
  is blocked until this is resolved. Cashiers never see cost figures on Returns.

**Stock** — two lines of the same item on one sale are now counted together
against what is on hand (before, 6 + 6 could be sold from 10).

**Passphrases** — an owner/manager passphrase must contain at least one letter
(any alphabet). Digits or symbols alone are refused, in any script.

**Manager dashboard** (owner and manager)
* Gross sales, refunds, net sales, receipts and average ticket for Today, Last 7
  days, Last 30 days or a custom range, for every employee; sales by day and top
  items. No costs or margins.

**Sign-in**
* Owner and manager passphrases need 8 characters including one letter;
  letters alone are fine.
* The till locks after **45 minutes** without anyone touching it (was 5).
  Background activity does not keep it awake.
* First-time setup: the enrollment screen has an **Open the enrollment code**
  button. The code is still unique to the computer, single-use and expires in
  24 hours.

**Look**
* New icons: Production is a red **G** with melted cheese; Training is yellow and
  black. The About box (click the version number in the sidebar) carries the
  developer credit; it appears nowhere else.
* Number boxes in New Purchase are readable on the Midnight theme.
